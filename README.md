## 🌟 About the Project

Invisible Thread is an interactive platform that explores hidden connections between locations and helps users understand how a disruption in one place may affect nearby facilities.

The project uses geographical mapping, data analysis, and interactive visualisation to explore possible disruption scenarios.

For our current implementation, we selected **healthcare infrastructure as a scenario**, focusing on mapped hospitals and pharmacies across Andhra Pradesh.

Our goal is to make geographical relationships easier to explore and identify nearby facilities that may be considered as potential alternatives.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

When a facility becomes unavailable, understanding the nearby alternatives and possible geographical impact can be challenging.

Invisible Thread helps users explore mapped facilities around a selected location and investigate how a hypothetical disruption may affect the surrounding area.

## 💡 Our Solution

Our platform allows users to explore healthcare facilities, select a location, define a search radius, and view other mapped facilities within that area.

Through interactive maps and scenario summaries, users can explore geographical relationships and identify locations that may require further investigation.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🗺️ **Interactive Geographical Mapping:** Explore mapped healthcare facilities and locations.
- ⚡ **Disruption Simulator:** Create hypothetical scenarios and examine their potential consequences.
- 🛣️ **Alternative Route Exploration:** View alternative road routes for a selected starting point.
- 🏥 **Healthcare Accessibility Exploration:** Identify nearby mapped facilities that may be worth investigating as alternatives.
- 🔗 **Impact and Connection Exploration:** Examine potential relationships between a disruption, nearby facilities and connected services.
- 📊 **Scenario Insights:** Review scenario summaries and comparisons to support further investigation.

**Scope note:** Results depend on the available data and implemented scenario logic. Nearby facilities are not guaranteed to be operational substitutes, and simulated impacts should not be interpreted as validated real-world predictions.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **Data Processing:** Pandas
- **Data Formats:** CSV, GeoJSON, JSON
- **Geographical Data:** OpenStreetMap

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Explore the mapped facilities.
2. Select a facility for a hypothetical disruption scenario.
3. Choose a geographical search radius.
4. The backend identifies mapped facilities within that radius.
5. View the results and explore potential alternatives.
