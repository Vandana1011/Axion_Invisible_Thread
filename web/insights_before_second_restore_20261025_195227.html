Interpretation & limitations
This dashboard reports hypothetical simulation outputs based on proximity-based candidate connections. Candidate connections are not verified operational dependencies. A zero-candidate result is a potential access/connectivity warning in this model, not proof that a hospital is isolated or unavailable in real life. These results do not establish actual service disruption or patient outcomes.
